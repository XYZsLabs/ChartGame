Playing ChartGame

The basics, before your next move.

How does ChartGame work?

You're shown a real historical stock or crypto chart. Make buy and sell decisions as the chart reveals bar by bar. At the end, your return is compared with a simple buy-and-hold strategy.

Is real money involved?

No. ChartGame is a simulation for practice and education. All trading uses virtual currency, so no real money is at risk.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day, all players get the same chart to trade. Results are ranked on a daily leaderboard so you can see how your skills compare.

Account & privacy

Getting started, starting over, and your data.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app automatically creates an anonymous account using Firebase Authentication. No email or phone number is required. Choose a display name, or use the random one assigned to you.

How do I reset my account?

Go to your profile in the app and use the reset account option. This deletes your Firebase account, profile photo, and follow relationships, then creates a fresh anonymous account with a new user ID. Your previous leaderboard entry may remain as a historical record.

What data does ChartGame collect?

Your display name, game performance stats, and daily challenge results are stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
